# Website editing conventions

- Link professor names to their verified official faculty page or personal academic website wherever practical, including biography, news, and publication author lists. This is the site owner's standing preference.
- Preserve existing valid links. Do not guess a URL or link a namesake when identity is uncertain.
- Keep page footers copyright-only. Use al-folio as the design reference and a blue accent palette. Preserve required asset licenses and source credits in project documentation.
- Use a paragraph-based biography with current affiliation, research interests, and concise prior research context. Avoid reducing it to a single sentence.
- Keep the simple publication layout with linked faculty/advisor names in the blue accent. Do not add invented topic illustrations or diagrams. Keep the independent publications page separate.
