Sudarshan Balaji

PhD student · University of Memphis

Hi! I’m a first-year PhD student at the University of Memphis, advised by Dr. Dipankar Dasgupta.

My research interests include federated learning, secure federated learning and its applications, computer vision, and large language and vision-language models (LLMs/VLMs).

Previously, I completed my MS in Data Science at the University of Memphis, where I worked as a research assistant with Dr. Deepak Venugopal. I hold a BE in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering from RV College of Engineering.

My work has explored how vision-language models respond to changes in the questions we ask them. I have also worked on educational data visualization through the ASTRA project and on NLP applications for clinical data at CharmHealth.
