PhD student · University of Memphis

Hi! I’m a first-year PhD student at the University of Memphis, advised by Dr. Dipankar Dasgupta.

My research interests include federated learning, secure federated learning and its applications, computer vision, and large language and vision-language models (LLMs/VLMs).

Previously, I completed my MS in Data Science at the University of Memphis and worked as a research assistant with Dr. Deepak Venugopal.
